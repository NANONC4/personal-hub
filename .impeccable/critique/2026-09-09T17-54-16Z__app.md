## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** Passes decisively. `WelcomeWindow` is original work: two-depth skyline of ~33 buildings, deterministically seeded lit panes, ~40% lit with ~30% of those breathing on 13–21s cycles, warm:cool 5:1. That statistical texture is why it reads as a city rather than decoration.

But the site is specific in atmosphere and generic in architecture. Under the window sits a conventional dev-portfolio skeleton — hero with gradient name, "Who am I?", tech stack, three project cards, bento services, pricing, FAQ, testimonials. `app/page.tsx` sections 1–5 are the canonical order of a thousand Next.js templates; the pixel styling is a skin over that order.

Three places where the declared world actually breaks: `/bio` renders on a light sky gradient (`app/bio/page.tsx:19`) in a system whose first Don't forbids a light mode; `/services` fields four competing accents with a violet CTA inside a sky card; `/contact` uses `amber-100` as border, fill and text, against the just-written rule that amber is light, never UI.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI exit 2, 57 findings (app 9, components 48): design-system-color x23, border-accent-on-rounded x7, ai-color-palette x6, gradient-text x5, bounce-easing x4, side-tab x4, design-system-font-size x4, gray-on-color x3, design-system-radius x1.

Browser detector injected on all 5 pages: `/` 95, `/portfolio` 18, `/services` 92, `/contact` 74, `/showreel` 173.

Caught by the detector and missed by the review: skipped heading levels on 3 pages (h1 to h3, h1 to h3, h2 to h4), `undersized-ui-text` 10px x32, `nested-cards` x40, `line-length` ~92ch on `/portfolio`, `kicker-above-heading` x6 on `/showreel`.

**Proven false positives.** 15 of 23 `design-system-color` are technology brand hexes in `TechBreakout.tsx:8–22`, embedded in the icon URL on the same line — 26% of the CLI total is this one construct. All 3 `gray-on-color` measure 7.28–8.07:1 (the rule matches the `slate-*` name rather than luminance). At least 3 more are double-counts.

`ai-color-palette` firing hard (52 on `/`, 72 on `/services`, 60 on `/contact`) is the committed identity, not a defect — but the spread between pages is real signal, and `/services` independently drew the "four competing accents" finding.

**Visual overlays.** Injection succeeded and console output was read on all 5 pages, but the live server was stopped per protocol. No overlay remains in the user's browser.

## Overall Impression

The window is real; the rest has not caught up. The most memorable thing is at the door, and walking through it lands on one empty screen before any content — spending the best peak and paying it off with a void, the inverse of peak-end.

Biggest single opportunity: halve the distance from landing to seeing actual work, without giving up the window.

## What's Working

1. **WelcomeWindow is a defensible moat** — statistical texture, not a scroll gimmick.
2. **The two-voice type system holds** — Silkscreen for headings/labels, Inter for body, never mixed; critically, Thai body copy is set in Inter rather than forced into a pixel face.
3. **The `/portfolio` irregular grid resists template-ness** — alternating 7/5 col-spans mean no two adjacent cards match. It will be genuinely good the moment it has images.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The Arsenal destroys its own content before the visitor arrives
`TechBreakout` sets `autoPlay = true` on mount (`components/TechBreakout.tsx:148`). The bricks are the tech stack; by the time a desktop visitor scrolls past a 220vh gate, hero, and About, the field is cleared (score already 0060 during About). The section answering "what can he do" currently answers nothing, with no fallback list — against PRODUCT.md Principle 1.
Fix: IntersectionObserver-gate the loop at 50% in view and pause on exit; make autoplay an attract mode that does not clear bricks; render a readable stack list beside the bezel at 0.75rem or larger.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

### [P0] The gate blanks navigation and traps every visitor for two screens
`WelcomeWindow` portals `fixed inset-0 z-[70]` to body; `TopNavbar` is `z-50`. For ~60% of the scroll budget nav is invisible and un-clickable. No skip, no click-to-open, no Esc; the layer is `aria-hidden`, so screen readers get 220vh of nothing. PRODUCT.md says most arrivals are mobile bio-link and the works zone is the substance — the structure makes the substance maximally distant.
Fix: raise nav to `z-[80]` with a translucent scrim; make the gate click-to-open and Esc-dismissible; cut the spacer from 220vh to ~140vh (sashes finish at p=0.64); `sessionStorage` the completion.
Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] One empty screen sits between the gate's payoff and the hero
The layer fades out at p~1.0 (~1.2 screens) but the `h-[220vh]` spacer pins the hero until 2.2 screens, producing a full viewport of nothing. It converts the strongest beat into an anticlimax and reads as a loading failure.
Fix: align the two, or cross-fade the window into the hero (moon into the gradient name, city lights into the existing skyline) so the peak lands on content.
Suggested command: `/impeccable animate`

### [P1] The accessibility commitment the product doc made is unmet
`prefers-reduced-motion` appears in 2 files; both canvas games, the steam, gradient loop, `KineticText`, `DiaTextReveal`, `BentoAnimations` and every `repeat: Infinity` ignore it. Only 3 files contain `focus-visible`; `app/faq/page.tsx:91` sets `focus:outline-none` with no replacement. Neither canvas has role/aria-label. `lang="en"` on Thai copy.
Fix: shared `useReducedMotion()` hook short-circuiting every infinite loop and both rAF loops; global `:focus-visible` ring in `globals.css` using late-night-pink; `lang="th"`; `role="img"` plus `aria-label` on each canvas.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] The bento grid is inert on mobile, and mobile is the primary arrival
`BentoGrid.tsx` body is `pointer-events-none`; the CTA is `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100`. No hover means no visible CTA and no reachable link. The Midnight Cafe card points at a bare `https://discord.gg/`.
Fix: show the CTA at rest below `md`; remove `pointer-events-none` or wrap the card in the link; supply the real invite or drop the card.
Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`

## Minor Observations

- `app/contact/page.tsx:85` labels the section "Verified Reviews" with a 5.0/5.0 badge — an affirmative claim the page cannot support, against PRODUCT.md Principle 5. Change it regardless of what happens to the review data.
- `/contact` leads with a full screen of testimonials before any contact channel. Invert it.
- Two `h1` on the home page (the gate's is aria-hidden). Demote it to a `div`.
- `/bio` is the only page with no Footer and no exit besides the top nav; `handleUnlockAndScroll` is defined and unused.
- `TechBreakout` fetches 15 icons from `cdn.simpleicons.org` at runtime; an ad blocker removes the only confirmation a brick broke. Inline them as SVG.
- Emoji in the hero badge and on `/contact` violate DESIGN.md's own no-emoji-as-icon rule.
- ~400px of empty space stacks between "Experience Full Showreel" and the bento grid; calm is the goal but this reads as a missing section.
- `/showreel`'s sticky bar is `z-[50]`, identical to `TopNavbar`.
- Footer `text-slate-600/50` on near-black measures **2.66:1**, below the 4.5:1 floor, and it is the line that closes every page — with no links, no contact, no next step.

## Questions to Consider
